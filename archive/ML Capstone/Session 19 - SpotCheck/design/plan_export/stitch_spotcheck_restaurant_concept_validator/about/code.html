The Data & Methodology

Built on 9,244 Bangalore restaurants from the canonical open dataset 'Zomato Bangalore Restaurants' by Himanshu Poddar (~2019).

shield

Zero post-launch leakage: We strictly isolate pre-opening variables—micro-location, format, cuisine combination, and intended price bracket. Customer review counts and historical upvotes are completely excluded from predictive inputs.

gavel

Limitations & Caveats

Honest AI
⚠️
Intangibles

Food taste, service warmth, culinary consistency, and kitchen hygiene cannot be measured by tabular algorithms.

⚠️
Survivor bias

Defunct or quietly closed outlets are naturally underrepresented in commercial restaurant listings.

⚠️
2019 baseline

Represents a solid structural operational baseline; does not explicitly reflect post-2022 raw ingredient inflation.

⚠️
Bangalore only

Hyper-calibrated exclusively to Bangalore micro-markets (Indiranagar, Koramangala, Whitefield, HSR, Jayanagar).

SpotCheck is an academic decision-support tool created for the MICA ML Capstone. Not affiliated with Zomato. No sign-up, no private data stored.
